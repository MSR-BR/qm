Terms of Use

Important conditions for using the QUANTUM interactive book and study tools.

Educational purpose

QUANTUM is an educational companion to the book Quantum Mechanics. It supports guided reading, practice, simulations, assessments, and optional account-based learning records.

Generated exercises

Automatically generated exercises and solutions are experimental teaching resources and may contain conceptual, mathematical, or pedagogical errors. They do not replace the reviewed book, course instruction, or independent verification.

Responsible use

Use the reporting tools to flag possible errors. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or third-party personal information.

Availability

Reviewed chapters and available features may change as the editorial process continues. Chapters marked as under review remain unavailable.

Contact and privacy

For questions or reports, contact marioreis@id.uff.br. Read the Privacy notice for information about account and study data.
