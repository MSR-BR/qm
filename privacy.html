Privacy

How QUANTUM handles information used for sign-in and optional study features.

Account and study information

QUANTUM uses Google sign-in to identify your account and enable optional study features, including bookmarks, favorites, progress, assessments, and learning rewards. During authentication, the app may receive basic information authorized by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile image. This information is used for authentication, minimal personalization, and synchronizing your study records across devices.

The app may store information generated during use, such as favorites, your last reading point, section progress, simulator activity, assessment attempts, and learning rewards. These records support study continuity and the app's learning features.

Technical services and local data

Data are processed using the project's technical infrastructure, including authentication, database, and hosting services. They are not sold or used for personalized advertising. Optional measurement with Google is described separately below.

Your browser may also keep local information such as the active session, preferences, and temporary interface state.

Optional choices

Optional analytics use Google Analytics to measure learning actions such as opening a chapter or marking a section completed. With your permission, Google uses browser identifiers and cookies; these measurements are pseudonymous, not anonymous. QUANTUM does not send your name, email address, account ID, search text, answers, generated content, or feedback text to Analytics. Approved campaign labels may be retained in this tab for up to 30 minutes to measure how visits reach the app. Our separate first-party event records are retained for up to 90 days; Google Analytics event data retention is configured to 14 months.

Advertising measurement is a separate, optional choice, off by default. If you allow it together with analytics, campaign/click identifiers and learning events may be shared with Google Ads to measure whether advertisements lead to study. A section completion means you marked it studied, not that mastery was verified. We do not enable personalized advertising, Google Signals, remarketing audiences, or enhanced conversions using your identity.

You may allow analytics without advertising measurement, or decline both without losing access, progress or points. Use here or the analytics preferences control in the reading app to change or withdraw permission. Withdrawal stops future collection and removes QUANTUM measurement cookies; it does not retroactively delete events already processed. For more information, see how Google uses information from partner sites.

Optional learning email updates require a separate choice and are off by default. You can change your communication preference in Privacy and communication. Choosing not to receive updates does not affect your points or access.

Your requests

To correct, review, or request deletion of data associated with your account, contact marioreis@id.uff.br. You can also revoke Google access in your Google Account settings.

For a description of how study data inform exercises and rewards, see the learning guide.
