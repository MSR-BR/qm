# Source-fidelity rule for QM content

All instructional content in the Quantum Mechanics app must be traceable to the assigned source book chapter.

- Preserve the book's notation, definitions, order of argument, and stated physical concepts.
- Do not introduce alternative symbols, supplementary derivations, examples, analogies, or interpretations unless the book itself presents them.
- When a concise explanation is needed, paraphrase only what the book states and keep the original mathematical notation.
- Verify equations, variable names, figures, captions, and referenced conditions against the corresponding PDF pages before publication.
- When a topic is absent or uncertain in the source, omit it and flag it for review rather than filling the gap from general background knowledge.

This rule applies to new and revised chapter pages, exercises, generated answers, figures, and captions.
