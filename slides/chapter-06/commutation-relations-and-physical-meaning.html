Commutation relations
From angular-momentum components to noncommuting operators
2 / 15
Guided reading

Section 6.1 begins with \(\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times\mathbf p\), writes its components with the Levi-Civita symbol and obtains the commutation relations of angular momentum.

Levi-Civita component form

For operator components, the book writes

\[\hat L_i=\epsilon_{ijk}\hat x_j\hat p_k\]

The indices \(i,j,k\) represent the Cartesian components \(x,y,z\).

Component vector
\[\mathbf L=L_x\hat x+L_y\hat y+L_z\hat z\]

The vector-product component form is left in the book as an exercise for the reader.

General commutator

The result of Section 6.1 is

\[[\hat L_u,\hat L_v]=i\hbar\epsilon_{uvw}\hat L_w\]

This compact equation contains the cyclic commutators.

Cyclic relations
\[[\hat L_x,\hat L_y]=i\hbar\hat L_z,\qquad [\hat L_z,\hat L_x]=i\hbar\hat L_y,\qquad [\hat L_y,\hat L_z]=i\hbar\hat L_x\]

The text states the immediate consequence: the components of angular momentum do not commute with one another.
