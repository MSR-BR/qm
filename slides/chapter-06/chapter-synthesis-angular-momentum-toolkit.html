Applying generalized uncertainty and closing the chapter

Guided reading

Appendix 6.B.3 applies the generalized uncertainty principle to angular momentum. Appendix 6.C then lists the exercises.

Angular-momentum uncertainty
\[(\Delta L_u)^2(\Delta L_v)^2\ge\frac14|\langle[\hat L_u,\hat L_v]\rangle|^2+|\mathrm{cov}(L_u,L_v)|^2\]

For the angular-momentum components discussed in the appendix, the covariance is zero.

\[\mathrm{cov}(L_u,L_v)=0\]
Final inequality
\[\Delta L_u\,\Delta L_v\ge\frac{\hbar}{2}|\epsilon_{uvw}\langle\hat L_w\rangle|\]
\[\Delta L_x\,\Delta L_y\ge\frac{\hbar^2}{2}|m|\]

The book asks the reader to think about the physical meaning of the last result.

Chapter synthesis

The chapter has one continuous route: define angular momentum, derive noncommuting components, select the commuting pair \(\hat L^2,\hat L_z\), use ladder operators to obtain \(l,m\), build matrices, then recover the same eigenvalue equations in position space.
