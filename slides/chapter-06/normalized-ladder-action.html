Chapter 6 · Item 6.8
Normalized ladder action
The coefficients that connect neighboring m states
8 / 15
Guided reading

After the spectrum is known, Section 6.2 determines the constants (c_pm) in the ladder action.

Start from the unnormalized action
\[\hat L_\pm|l,m\rangle=c_\pm|l,m\pm1\rangle\]

The coefficient is found by taking the norm of the state after the ladder operation.

Use the adjoint relation
\[\langle l,m|\hat L_\pm^\dagger\hat L_\pm|l,m\rangle=|c_\pm|^2\]
\[\hat L_\pm^\dagger\hat L_\pm=\hat L_\mp\hat L_\pm=\hat L^2-\hat L_z^2\mp\hbar\hat L_z\]
Coefficient
\[|c_\pm|=\sqrt{l(l+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,\hbar\]

The coefficient becomes zero at the proper endpoint of the ladder.

Corollary 6.9
\[\hat L_\pm|l,m\rangle=\sqrt{l(l+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,\hbar|l,m\pm1\rangle\]

The book calls these raising and lowering operators because they alter the quantum number \(m\) by one unit.
