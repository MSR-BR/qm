Angular momentum spectrum
Definitions of l and m and the eigenvalue equations
7 / 15
Guided reading

Corollaries 6.6 to 6.8 complete the spectrum. The upper endpoint is reached after an integer number of ladder steps, and \(b\) is written as (mhbar).

Counting ladder steps

Applying \(\hat L_+\) repeatedly takes \(|a,b_{\min}\rangle\) to \(|a,b_{\max}\rangle\). If this takes \(n\) steps, then

\[b_{\max}=b_{\min}+n\hbar\]
\[\frac{b_{\max}}{\hbar}=\frac{n}{2}\]

Definition 6.3 writes \(n/2=l\), so \(l\) can be integer or half-integer.

Eigenvalue of L squared
\[a=b_{\max}(b_{\max}+\hbar)=l(l+1)\hbar^2\]

This is Corollary 6.7.

Definition of m

Definition 6.4 writes

\[b=m\hbar\]

The allowed values are

\[-l\le m\le +l,\qquad m=-l,-l+1,\ldots,l-1,l\]

There are \(2l+1\) possible values of \(m\).

Final eigenvalue equations
\[\hat L^2|l,m\rangle=l(l+1)\hbar^2|l,m\rangle\]
\[\hat L_z|l,m\rangle=m\hbar|l,m\rangle\]

Operators \(\hat L_x\) and \(\hat L_y\) are constructed from \(\hat L_\pm\), but they are not diagonal in the \(|l,m\rangle\) basis.
