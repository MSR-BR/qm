Box 6.1: commutator proof
The Levi-Civita derivation of the angular-momentum algebra
3 / 15
Guided reading

Box 6.1 gives the detailed proof of the commutation relations. The page keeps the proof skeleton and leaves the line-by-line tensor algebra in the book.

Starting point
\[[\hat L_u,\hat L_v]=[\epsilon_{uab}\hat x_a\hat p_b,\epsilon_{vcd}\hat x_c\hat p_d]\]

The Levi-Civita symbols collect the Cartesian components of the two vector products.

Canonical input

The proof uses the generalized position-momentum relation

\[[\hat x_i,\hat p_j]=i\hbar\delta_{ij}\]

This is the same noncommutation responsible for the uncertainty principle.

Intermediate contraction
\[[\hat L_u,\hat L_v]=i\hbar\{-\epsilon_{uab}\epsilon_{vbd}\hat x_a\hat p_d+\epsilon_{uab}\epsilon_{vca}\hat x_c\hat p_b\}\]

The Kronecker deltas contract one index of the second Levi-Civita symbol.

Recovered result

After the Levi-Civita identity is applied, the proof obtains

\[[\hat L_u,\hat L_v]=i\hbar(\hat x_u\hat p_v-\hat x_v\hat p_u)=i\hbar\epsilon_{uvw}\hat L_w\]
