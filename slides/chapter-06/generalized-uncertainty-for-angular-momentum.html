Schrödinger uncertainty and Schwartz inequality
The generalized uncertainty relation applied to angular momentum
8 / 9
Guided reading

This item follows Appendix 6.B. It first states the Schwartz inequality, then the Schrödinger generalized uncertainty principle, and finally applies it to angular momentum.

Schwartz inequality

For two vectors \(|n\rangle\) and \(|m\rangle\), the appendix obtains

\[\langle n|n\rangle\langle m|m\rangle\ge |\langle n|m\rangle|^2\]

This is the inequality used to derive the generalized uncertainty relation.

Operator fluctuations
\[|a\rangle=\delta\hat a|\psi\rangle=(\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle)|\psi\rangle\]
\[|b\rangle=\delta\hat b|\psi\rangle=(\hat B-\langle\hat B\rangle)|\psi\rangle\]
Generalized uncertainty
\[\operatorname{cov}(A,B)={1\over2}\langle\{\delta\hat a,\delta\hat b\}\rangle={1\over2}\langle\{\hat A,\hat B\}\rangle-\langle\hat A\rangle\langle\hat B\rangle\]
\[(\Delta A)^2(\Delta B)^2\ge {1\over4}|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle|^2+|\operatorname{cov}(A,B)|^2\]
Angular momentum
\[(\Delta L_u)^2(\Delta L_v)^2\ge {1\over4}|\langle[\hat L_u,\hat L_v]\rangle|^2+|\operatorname{cov}(L_u,L_v)|^2\]
\[\operatorname{cov}(L_u,L_v)=0\]
\[\Delta L_u\Delta L_v\ge {\hbar\over2}|\epsilon_{uvw}\langle\hat L_w\rangle|\]
\[\Delta L_x\Delta L_y\ge {\hbar^2\over2}|m|\]
Physical reading

The appendix notes that \(\Delta L_z\) can be zero for a state with definite \(m\), without violating the uncertainty principle. The nonzero product for \(\Delta L_x\Delta L_y\) is controlled by the commutator and by \(m\).
