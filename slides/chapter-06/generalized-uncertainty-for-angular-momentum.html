Appendix 6.A: Levi-Civita symbol

The Levi-Civita symbol is defined as a rank-3 tensor with values \(+1\), \(-1\), or \(0\), according to the permutation of its indices.

\[\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{imn}=\delta_{jm}\delta_{kn}-\delta_{jn}\delta_{km}\]

The book also states the Einstein notation convention: a repeated index represents a sum over that index.

Appendix 6.B.1: Schwartz inequality
\[\langle n|n\rangle\langle m|m\rangle\ge|\langle n|m\rangle|^2\]

This inequality is the tool used in the book to derive the generalized uncertainty principle.

Covariance
\[\mathrm{cov}(A,B)=\frac12\langle\{\delta\hat a,\delta\hat b\}\rangle\]
\[\mathrm{cov}(A,B)=\frac12\langle\{\hat A,\hat B\}\rangle-\langle\hat A\rangle\langle\hat B\rangle\]

Here \(\delta\hat a=\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle\) and \(\delta\hat b=\hat B-\langle\hat B\rangle\).

Schrodinger uncertainty principle
\[(\Delta A)^2(\Delta B)^2\ge\frac14|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle|^2+|\mathrm{cov}(A,B)|^2\]

The book also writes this result using the covariance matrix \(\Sigma\).
