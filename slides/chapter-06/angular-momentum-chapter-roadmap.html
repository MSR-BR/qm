Guided reading

Chapter 6 studies angular momentum as a fundamental physical quantity. The book's stated objective is to present the formal algebra of angular momentum, which can be orbital or spin, and its consequences.

Chapter objective

The chapter starts from angular momentum and develops the algebra needed to obtain eigenvalues, eigenvectors, matrix representations and position-space representations.

The introduction emphasizes that this chapter is fundamental for the next chapter, where addition of angular momenta is studied.

Starting definition

The first equation of the chapter is the classical definition

\[\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times\mathbf p\]

It is followed by the component form of \(\mathbf L\).

\[\mathbf L=L_x\hat x+L_y\hat y+L_z\hat z\]
