Degenerate case: first-order energy

REDUCED PERTURBATIONS AND THE CORRECT ZEROTH-ORDER VECTORS
6 / 10

Guided reading

Projecting the total eigenvalue equation onto \(A\) and \(B\) produces reduced perturbations. Within a degeneracy domain, the eigenvalues of the reduced perturbation are the first energy corrections.

Projected vectors and perturbations

Define the components of the exact vector and the reduced perturbations.

\[\hat A\lvert n\rangle=\lvert n_a\rangle,\qquad \hat B\lvert n\rangle=\lvert n_b\rangle,\] \[\hat W_A=\hat A\hat W\hat A,\qquad \hat W_B=\hat B\hat W\hat B.\]

Meaning

The eigenvectors of \(\hat W_B\), expressed in the degenerate subspace, are the correct \(\lvert n_b^{(0)}\rangle\). Their eigenvalues are \(E_{n_b}^{(1)}\). The same construction applies to every degeneracy domain.

Vector correction

The first correction to the vectors is developed in Chapter 8. Its construction is not repeated here; it restores the components outside and within the selected degeneracy domain while preserving the \(\lambda\to0\) limit.

First-order equation in \(B\)

Expanding the projected equation and collecting \(\lambda^0\) and \(\lambda^1\) yields:

\[(\hat H_0-E_{n_b}^{(0)})\hat B\lvert n_b^{(0)}\rangle=0,\] \[(\hat W_B-E_{n_b}^{(1)}\hat B)\lvert n_b^{(0)}\rangle=0.\]

Example 8.6

For the degenerate pair in Example 8.5, the \(B\)-reduced perturbation has off-diagonal entries \(2\). It gives the first-order corrections and selected combinations.

\[E_{1b}^{(1)}=-2,\qquad E_{2b}^{(1)}=+2,\] \[\lvert1_b^{(0)}\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}\left[-\lvert2^{(0)}\rangle+\lvert3^{(0)}\rangle\right],\qquad \lvert2_b^{(0)}\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}\left[\lvert2^{(0)}\rangle+\lvert3^{(0)}\rangle\right].\]
