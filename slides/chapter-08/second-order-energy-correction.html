Non-degenerate case: second-order energy and vectors

SECOND-ORDER SHIFTS, NORMALIZATION, AND MIXING
4 / 10
Guided reading

The second-order energy follows from the \(\lambda^2\) equation after projection on \(\langle n^{(0)}\rvert\). The second correction to the vector contains a normalization term, diagonal-off-diagonal terms, and paths through intermediate states.

Second-order energy

Hermiticity of \(\hat W\) turns the product of matrix elements into an absolute square.

\[E_n^{(2)}=\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{\left\lvert\langle m^{(0)}\vert\hat W\vert n^{(0)}\rangle\right\rvert^2}{E_n^{(0)}-E_m^{(0)}}.\]
Example 8.3

For the same three-dimensional example, the energy corrections are obtained directly from the non-zero off-diagonal elements.

\[E_1^{(2)}=-\frac12,\qquad E_2^{(2)}=\frac12,\qquad E_3^{(2)}=0,\] \[\lvert1^{(2)}\rangle=-\frac18\lvert1^{(0)}\rangle,\qquad \lvert2^{(2)}\rangle=-\frac18\lvert2^{(0)}\rangle.\]
Second-order vector

With \(W_{ab}=\langle a^{(0)}\vert\hat W\vert b^{(0)}\rangle\) and \(E_{ab}=E_a^{(0)}-E_b^{(0)}\), the result is:

\[\begin{aligned}\lvert n^{(2)}\rangle={}&-\frac12\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{|W_{mn}|^2}{E_{nm}^2}\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle-\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{W_{nn}W_{mn}}{E_{nm}^2}\lvert m^{(0)}\rangle\\&+\sum_{m\ne n}\sum_{l\ne n}\frac{W_{ml}W_{ln}}{E_{nm}E_{nl}}\lvert m^{(0)}\rangle.\end{aligned}\]
Combined result

Keeping terms through second order reproduces the small-\(\lambda\) expansion of the exact example.

\[E_1=1-\frac{\lambda^2}{2},\qquad E_2=3+\frac{\lambda^2}{2},\qquad E_3=-2+\lambda,\] \[\lvert1\rangle=\lvert1^{(0)}\rangle-\frac{\lambda}{2}\lvert2^{(0)}\rangle-\frac{\lambda^2}{8}\lvert1^{(0)}\rangle.\]
