QHO: raising and lowering operators
An alternative construction of the oscillator eigenstates
4 / 14
Guided reading

Section 4.1.3 rewrites the harmonic-oscillator Hamiltonian in terms of two operators. Their action changes the quantum number by one unit, and repeated application of the raising operator constructs the stationary eigenfunctions from the ground state.

Rewriting the Hamiltonian

With \(\xi=\alpha x\) and \(\alpha=\sqrt{m\omega/\hbar}\), the book defines

\[\hat a^+=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\left(-\frac{d}{d\xi}+\hat\xi\right),\qquad \hat a^-=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\left(\frac{d}{d\xi}+\hat\xi\right).\]

The Hamiltonian then takes the compact form

\[\hat H=\hbar\omega\left(\hat a^+\hat a^-+\frac12\right).\]
Number operator

For an energy eigenfunction \(\hat H\psi_n(x)=E_n\psi_n(x)\), define

\[\hat N=\hat a^+\hat a^-,\qquad \hat N\psi_n(x)=n\psi_n(x).\]

Thus the oscillator energy \(E_n=\hbar\omega(n+1/2)\) is associated with the eigenvalue \(n\) of \(\hat N\).

Properties used in the construction
\[[\hat a^-,\hat a^+]=1,\qquad [\hat H,\hat a^\pm]=\pm\hbar\omega\hat a^\pm,\qquad [\hat N,\hat a^\pm]=\pm\hat a^\pm.\]

Also, the operators are non-Hermitian conjugates of one another: \((\hat a^\pm)^\dagger=\hat a^\mp\).

Effect on the eigenfunctions

Using the commutators, the book obtains

\[\hat H(\hat a^\pm\psi_n)=(E_n\pm\hbar\omega)(\hat a^\pm\psi_n),\qquad \hat N(\hat a^\pm\psi_n)=(n\pm1)(\hat a^\pm\psi_n).\]

Accordingly, \(\hat a^+\) is the raising operator and \(\hat a^-\) the lowering operator.

Coefficients of the ladder action

The action is first written as \(\hat a^+\psi_n=c_n\psi_{n+1}\) and \(\hat a^-\psi_n=d_n\psi_{n-1}\). The normalization calculation in Box 4.6 gives \(d_n=\sqrt n\) and \(c_n=\sqrt{n+1}\):

\[\hat a^-\psi_n=\sqrt n\,\psi_{n-1},\qquad \hat a^+\psi_n=\sqrt{n+1}\,\psi_{n+1}.\]
Eigenstates from the ground state

Successive application of the raising operator gives \((\hat a^+)^n\psi_0=\sqrt{n!}\,\psi_n\), hence

\[\psi_n=\frac{(\hat a^+)^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\psi_0.\]

For the ground state, \(\hat a^-\psi_0=0\) gives the first-order equation in Eq. (4.86) and the normalized result

\[\psi_0(x)=\left(\frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}\exp\!\left(-\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}x^2\right).\]
Connection with the previous solution

The ground-state wave function obtained here is the same result obtained in Section 4.1.2 through the Hermite differential equation. This subsection presents the operator construction as an alternative mathematical approach.
