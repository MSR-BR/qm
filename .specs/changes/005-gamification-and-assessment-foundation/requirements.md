# Requirements

1. Only Chapters 1–7 may contribute progress or rewards.
2. A learner may receive completion points once per reviewed section.
3. The browser must never calculate or directly write official point totals.
4. Server-side events must be authenticated, idempotent, and validated against the canonical publication registry and chapter catalogue.
5. All learner data must remain private under RLS; no leaderboard is in scope.
6. Study journey must show points, current streak, best streak, and a clearly marked assessment placeholder.
7. No questions, answers, or physics content may be invented for assessments.
