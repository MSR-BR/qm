# Requirements

- Separate learning, behavior, experience, implementation fidelity, and equity/safety outcomes.
- Inherit the shared learning contract, privacy rules, security profile, and Pó Mágico `S3_SENSITIVE` overlay without weakening them.
- Record eligibility, exposure, action, reward/deduplication, and opt-out for each mechanic.
- Require baseline plus delayed or changed-form evidence before claims about retention, transfer, or mastery.
- Report sample, exposure, attrition, missing data, effect size, uncertainty, adverse effects, and limitations.
- Keep GA4 and product analytics outside the authoritative learning ledger.
- Migrate optional e-mail consent to affirmative opt-in; do not preselect it.
- Enforce server-side caps, quiet hours, pause, unsubscribe, and reviewed-content eligibility.
- Prohibit threatened point loss, rank/mastery pressure, and artificial urgency.
- Keep sender activation, provider secrets, scheduled remote jobs, and deployment behind separate explicit authorization.
