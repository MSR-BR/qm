# Acceptance criteria

- [x] Research-backed principles and configurable defaults are explicitly distinguished.
- [x] The policy answers what to recommend, why, when, how often, and how errors/correct answers change scheduling.
- [x] Chapter assessment and Daily Challenge have distinct purposes and share one learner model.
- [x] The event contract is server-authoritative, atomic, idempotent, private, and testable.
- [x] E-mail and first-login consent follow affirmative opt-in rules.
- [x] AI use is limited to reviewed sources and produces an explainable recommendation.
- [x] The skill validates successfully and is reusable outside QUANTUM.
- [x] Supplied studies are appraised with sample, outcome, limitation, and design consequence rather than cited as undifferentiated support.
- [x] Learning, behavior, experience, implementation fidelity, and equity/safety outcomes are separated.
- [x] Structural mechanics require mechanism cards; points cannot change grades or assessment difficulty, and public individual rankings are not the default.
- [x] Physics activities include prediction/modeling, reasoning, interpretation, near transfer, and delayed retrieval where applicable.
