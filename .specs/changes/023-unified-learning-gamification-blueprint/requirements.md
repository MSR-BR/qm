# Requirements

- Base recommendations on retrieval practice, spacing, successive relearning, feedback, confidence, and prerequisite structure.
- Maintain an academic evidence register that separates learning, behavior, experience, and equity outcomes and records study limitations.
- Require a mechanism card for each structural game element and treat instructional content design as primary.
- Distinguish local section practice, chapter assessment, Daily Challenge, guided remediation, simulator activity, and long-term mastery.
- Define how errors, correct low-confidence answers, correct high-confidence answers, hints, and elapsed time affect the next recommendation.
- Define a graduated hint policy and a feedback contract linked to reviewed source material.
- Define a server-authoritative, idempotent event ledger and derived learner profile.
- Prevent negative points, shame loops, visit-only farming, and unlimited reward repetition.
- Prevent points from purchasing answers, grades, exemptions, or easier scoring; keep public individual rankings disabled by default.
- Define a versioned points policy, missions, badges, next actions, and mastery evidence without letting rewards replace learning goals.
- Define privacy, explainability, e-mail frequency caps, quiet hours, and explicit opt-in rules.
- Restrict AI-generated recommendations and exercises to reviewed, published content and the exercise-source manifest.
- Provide validation gates for pedagogy, content fidelity, security, data integrity, accessibility, observability, and product outcomes.
- Provide an evaluation contract covering baseline knowledge, implementation fidelity, delayed/transfer evidence, attrition, effect sizes, uncertainty, and adverse effects.
- Store the complete skill package under `.specs/blueprints/adaptive-learning-gamification/` and validate it with the Codex skill validator.

## Risks

- Fixed schedules or point values may be mistaken for universal scientific constants; defaults must be configurable and labeled as product policy.
- A single opaque mastery score can hide different misconceptions; keep evidence at the skill/concept level.
- AI recommendations without source constraints can introduce unreviewed physics.
- Engagement optimization without safeguards can create manipulative notification patterns.
- Engagement or positive attitudes may be mistaken for conceptual learning; the outcome taxonomy must remain explicit in product analytics and release claims.
- Metadata-only reviews, retracted papers, and internally inconsistent reports can create false confidence if not quality-screened.
