# Requirements

- Begin after the final domain, canonical SEO, Search Console ownership, and sitemap are operational.
- The owner reviews the public application in English, as a prospective international learner, on desktop and mobile.
- Cover the landing and navigation experience, chapters 1–7, locked chapters 8–13, search, simulators, exercises, sign-in and return flows, learner area, reporting flow, accessibility presentation, and visible SEO-facing pages.
- Record observations as reproducible issues or approved outcomes; preserve screenshots or URLs when helpful.
- Fixes must remain within approved scope and follow the existing CPD rule.
- This review is a product-acceptance step, not a substitute for the independent C17 audit.
