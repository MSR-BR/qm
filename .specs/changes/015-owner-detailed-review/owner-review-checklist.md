# C15 — Owner review checklist after the C33 release

Use `https://quantummechanicsbook.app` with the QUANTUM Google account. Review at
desktop width (about 1440 px) and a real phone or narrow 390/320 px viewport.
Do not share credentials, tokens or another learner's records in reports. The
September screenshots are historical; record any current failure with its URL,
device, time, action and exact visible message. Optional analytics may remain off.

| Journey | Desktop and mobile acceptance check |
| --- | --- |
| Public entry and chapter navigation | Landing explains the book; menu and header controls fit without horizontal scrolling; Chapters 1–7 open and Chapters 8–13 remain under editorial review. |
| Reviewed section and mathematics | A Chapter 1 and Chapter 7 section show readable MathJax, source/context and an accessible path to the next activity. Marking a section studied survives reload. |
| Simulator | Open a reviewed simulator, complete predict → manipulate → compare → reflect, and confirm the private Journey records the guided cycle. Opening or refreshing alone must not award points or imply mastery. |
| Chapter assessment | Start a reviewed chapter assessment, answer and submit once. Confirm one result, saved history after reload, clear feedback and a source link. A repeated tap must not duplicate errors or rewards. |
| Daily Challenge and guided review | Prepare a challenge from eligible reviewed material, use a hint or make an error, inspect feedback and changed-form retry. No missed-day penalty or extra daily completion bonus. |
| Saved exercise and favorite | Save an eligible exercise, favorite it, open it from Favorites and check formatted statement, solution, reviewed source, share/report actions. No raw `\\(…\\)` delimiters in cards. |
| Study Journey and rewards | After an eligible completion, check points, progress, due review and the explained next action; reload and compare. A score/streak alone must not say “mastered”. Distinguish unavailable data from zero. |
| Sign-in, privacy and sign-out | Google-only login works; required notice and optional email preference are separate, optional email defaults off, settings can be revisited, session persists after reload, then sign-out removes private data from view. Learning-email delivery is currently disabled even if preference is on. |
| Help and research limits | `help.html` explains source eligibility, exercise selection, different activity modes, rewards, AI limits, privacy and references without claiming that QUANTUM's effectiveness has been established. |

For each failure, classify **blocking** (data loss, wrong reward, privacy/security,
inaccessible core task), **major** (journey unusable/confusing), or **minor**
(copy/visual). A pass on one device does not certify the other. Automated public
browser smoke and C33 managed tests supplement, but do not replace, this
signed-in owner review or manual screen-reader/200% zoom checks.
