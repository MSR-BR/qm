# Notes

Initial point values preserve the recognizable TERMO adapter but do not measure learning. Evidence determines review/mastery; points are a transparent motivational projection with anti-farming limits.

The locally implemented contract is deliberately conservative:

- an assessment has three reviewed concept checkpoints per published chapter;
- a single success, a single session, a single representation, assisted work, or a
  simulator open cannot establish mastery;
- each concept needs at least two correct, unaided retrievals in distinct sessions
  and representations separated by at least 24 hours; a chapter needs all three
  represented concepts;
- Daily Challenge uses only reviewed material the learner has studied and awards no
  XP; missing a day never removes points or breaks a punitive streak;
- guided review is rewarded at most once per São Paulo local day and twice per
  chapter in a rolling seven-day window; its focused retry inherits eligibility;
- assessments award 30 XP once per learner/chapter, eligible review and retry award
  10 XP each, chapter mastery awards 80 XP once, and guided simulator evidence
  awards zero XP;
- optional AI may reorder only already eligible actions. It cannot introduce a
  question, unlock content, score an answer, grant mastery, or bypass the
  deterministic fallback.

These values are an auditable starting policy, not a claim of measured learning
effectiveness. C32 owns calibration and academic evaluation. C31 owns the stable
public methodology explanation.
