# Requirements

- Provide an external Google Books preview link in the public header.
- Let any visitor submit one anonymous app rating and optional feedback.
- Let the responsible account manage ratings and prepare audience campaigns.
- Let signed-in users control legal acknowledgement and email-update consent.
- Protect every new persistence table with RLS and server-side authorization.
- Do not send email unless the Resend credential and verified sender are configured.
- Do not add a hosted book-PDF delivery feature.
