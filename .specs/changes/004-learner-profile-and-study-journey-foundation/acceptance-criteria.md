# Acceptance criteria

- A signed-in learner can reopen the most recently studied reviewed section from Study journey.
- A signed-in learner can mark a reviewed section completed and reopen it if needed.
- The journey summarizes section and chapter completion using only the published registry.
- Anonymous visitors can still read content but receive no private progress record.
- All progress rows are accessible only to their owner through RLS.
- Chapters 8–13 are excluded from writes, completion totals, and journey navigation.
- All user-facing app text is in English.
