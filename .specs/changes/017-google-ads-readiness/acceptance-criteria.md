# Acceptance criteria

- Campaign brief and conversion plan are approved by the user.
- Measurement is confirmed in GA4 and, when chosen, Google Ads.
- Each ad group has approved landing pages, keywords, negatives, ad assets, and budget guardrails.
- Consent/privacy choices are documented before relevant tracking features are enabled.
- A launch checklist, monitoring cadence, stop conditions, and rollback/pause procedure are ready.
- No spend or campaign publication occurs without an explicit user instruction to launch.
