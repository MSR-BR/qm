Coupled Bases and Clebsch-Gordan Matrix

Choose up to four angular momenta \(l_i\). A value \(l_i=0\) means that subspace is absent. The builder constructs the coupling route step by step, then shows the local basis, the coupled basis and the Clebsch-Gordan matrix \(U\).

Original calculator · Quantum Mechanics book-app · Prof. Mario Reis
Inputs
The calculator repeatedly applies the Chapter 7 condition \(|l_a-l_b|\le l\le l_a+l_b\). The matrix \(U\) is written with coupled-basis rows and local-basis columns.
